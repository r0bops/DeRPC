Free demo · Ethereum mainnet

Your operators. Your verified reads.

Every read goes through Anon-RPC (the tor-js worker) to operators you choose, and is checked against a light client before it is shown. Reads that can't be proven are refused, and nothing ever falls back to a direct connection. No wallet or deposit is needed for free reads. Operators still see the query content. Also: integrator example · network check.

      4 · Verified reads
      
      
      
        Each value is proven with eth_getProof at the verified finalized block, through the worker and
        your operators. A repeat at the same block comes from the on-device cache. Public addresses only.
