Free demo · Ethereum mainnet

Your operators. Your verified reads.

Every read goes through Anon-RPC (the tor-js worker) to operators you choose, and is checked against a light client before it is shown. Reads that can't be proven are refused, and nothing ever falls back to a direct connection.

No wallet or deposit is needed for free reads. Operators still see the query content. Also: integrator example · network check.

    Verified reads

    Each value is proven with eth_getProof at the verified finalized block, through the worker and your operators. A repeat at the same block comes from the on-device cache. Public addresses only.

    ReadResultViaTime

    The simulated lying operator is part of this demo, not a real operator: it makes a real request through Tor to one of your chosen operators, then inflates the balances it returns. Rejecting it is local evidence only.
